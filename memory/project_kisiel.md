The project is a LaTeX proposal for CENG318 Human-Computer Interaction course at IYTE.

**Project:** Kişisel — a widget-based adaptive news aggregation and social curation platform (desktop-first, React/TypeScript, NestJS backend, AI summarization via OpenAI GPT-4o-mini).

**Why:** Course assignment with a specific template format from the professor.

**How to apply:** The proposal lives at docs/proposal/main.tex + docs/proposal/references.bib. Compile with XeLaTeX (not pdflatex) due to fontspec/Helvetica Neue. Full compile cycle: xelatex → bibtex → xelatex → xelatex.
